## What the expansion contains

Aurora Crown combines a jade upper dome with violet interior stars and cooler late color. Ruby Dahlia has twelve clustered ruby/rose petals around champagne stars. Sapphire Saturn separates a blue sphere from a larger tilted golden orbit in depth. Phoenix Palm uses eleven rising amber branches with traveling parents that split into rose leaves. Opal Supernova sends seven palette-distinct carriers into non-recursive child blossoms spaced at least 0.7 seconds apart.

Both 3D and Canvas compatibility rendering consume the same bounded simulation and evolving colors. Reservations include unborn branches and carrier children. Original catalog indices remain unchanged. The new selection panel shows five readable targets per collection instead of squeezing ten into one row. Collection switching remembers the most recent style in that session; the selected style persists after reload. Keyboard 6–9 and 0 extend the original 1–5 bindings.

The single-press launch, duplicate rejection, immutable committed rocket, next-family selection, manual takeover, pause ownership, offline caching, readable recovery, protected/transparent output, Ultra default and no-autoplay sound are preserved.
