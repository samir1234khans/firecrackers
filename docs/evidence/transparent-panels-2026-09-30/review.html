Reading these captures

  • PNGs are copied byte-for-byte, without cropping, scaling, overlays or retouching.
  • The panel test blocks service workers; its visible offline-storage notice is part of that controlled test context.
  • Scrollable panels are captured at their initial scroll position. Additional controls are reached through their internal scroll area.
  • This is interface evidence, not a WebGPU or photoreal graphics comparison. The recovery image intentionally has no renderer.

The thumbnails scale each whole image for browsing. The linked PNGs and their recorded hashes preserve the original capture bytes.
