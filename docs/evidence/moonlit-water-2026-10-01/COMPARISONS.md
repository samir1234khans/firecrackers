# Matched moonlit-water comparisons

Each pair uses seed `20260916`, Ultra quality, native WebGPU on this PC, reduced flashes enabled, sound disabled and the same viewport and logical time. The scene is reset through the normal UI after authored assets become active. These are deterministic captures, not frame-rate or physical-phone measurements.

Baseline: production `2026-10-01.4`, fingerprint `965c6eaa6ebd5aef2f5922831504a6bbe1c3879e24a26532432af279f5e09d51`. Candidate identity and full per-capture diagnostics are retained in [after.json](comparisons/after.json); baseline diagnostics are in [before.json](comparisons/before.json).

The moon path follows the composed moon, broad dark swells remain readable between launches, and contacts follow the sampled hull poses. The planar camera reflects the real world: high bursts whose mirror falls behind the foreground quay do not acquire an artificially relocated second burst. Their light still catches bounded wave facets.

Review the pairs at full size for repeating bands or rings, horizon shimmer, clipping seams, contact halos, unstable composition and loss of firework identity.
