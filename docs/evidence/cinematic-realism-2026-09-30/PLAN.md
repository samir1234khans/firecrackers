1. Capture release 2026-09-30.1 at identical seed, camera sizes and effect ages in hardware WebGPU/WebGL. Record short real-time frame cadence separately from deterministic visual checkpoints.
2. Build an original generated panoramic distant sky/shore texture with a quiet central sky, sparse warm shoreline lights and no baked fireworks/reflections. Load progressively with procedural fallback; fit its horizon to the measured waterline at desktop and portrait sizes.
3. Use Blender 5.2.1 to refine original animated fuse/motor/burst smoke density and gradient atlases. Preserve three families, 16 frames, atlas dimensions, editable masters and source scripts. Inspect representative animation frames, reopen sources and validate browser loading.
4. Refine TSL stars/trails with continuous connections, antialiased bright cores, soft halos and age-dependent width/color. Keep particle limits, interleaved instance buffers and seeded trajectories intact. Shade smoke with dark cores and locally illuminated rims.
5. Improve water with layered coherent ripples, rough reflection fragments, distance-dependent blur, sparse shore-light glints and bounded actual-effect reflection pass. Light the terrace from visible burst events and tune roughness/contact without overexposing the scene.
6. Compare before/after Willow, Saturn and Supernova at matched seeds and times; inspect early, peak and late phases on desktop/portrait. Refine demonstrated issues. Run all-ten regressions, transparent/reduced-flash paths, missing assets, offline, hardware WebGPU and forced WebGL/Canvas.
7. Publish isolated Cloudflare preview, run release CI, merge and deploy the qualified build through the existing route. Record fingerprint and rollback. Physical phone/thermal qualification remains separate from desktop hardware and browser emulation.
