# Original water normals v005

`masters/waterfront-v005.blend` is a scoped refinement of the inventoried v004
master. It changes only the `Water preview` material to a new packed normal map
and retains all 77 objects, including the 48 terrace objects. The original v004
master and its maps are preserved.

`public/art/water-normal-v005.png` contains an original, deterministic 512 × 512
normal field generated by Blender's embedded NumPy. It combines 96 integer
Fourier components over three wavelength bands with 16 smooth periodic domain
warp components. Crossing wavelets and wind direction remain coherent without
the previous three-wave repetition.

| Property | Measured result |
| --- | --- |
| PNG size | 230,518 bytes |
| Seed | 528319 |
| Color space | Non-Color, linear tangent-space data |
| Channels | RGB normalized XYZ encoded from −1…1 to 0…1; alpha 1 |
| Slope magnitude median | 0.06753 |
| Slope magnitude 95th percentile | 0.13986 |
| Maximum slope magnitude | 0.23058 |
| Quantized normal Z minimum | 0.97647 |
| Quantized normal length maximum error | 0.00462 |
| Continuous field periodicity error | Less than 5 × 10⁻¹⁵ on both axes |

The Fourier coefficients and warp terms are recorded in
`renders/water-v005/generation-receipt.json`. Soft slope saturation limits rare
peaks rather than clipping normals to their channel limits. The field is
periodic at one whole tile in both axes; adjacent edge pixels need not equal
because they occupy different sample positions in the continuous tile.

## Reproduce and inspect

```powershell
$waterBlenderExe = 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe'
& $waterBlenderExe --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python 'assets-source\blender\build_water_v005.py' -- --build
& $waterBlenderExe --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python 'assets-source\blender\build_water_v005.py' -- --pilot
& $waterBlenderExe --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python 'assets-source\blender\build_water_v005.py' -- --verify
```

The master exposes repeat scale, normal strength, and water roughness through
named material nodes. The pilot uses a temporary review camera and light sources,
a tighter .06 roughness lobe, and 1.0 normal strength to reveal the normal response
at 640 × 420. It renders in Cycles CPU with 48 samples. These review overrides
are not saved; the master retains .19 roughness and .8 normal strength.

The map and material pilot were visually inspected. The final master was reopened
in a fresh Blender process and the exported PNG reloaded independently. Verification
confirmed finite normalized data, all four color channels in bounds, opaque alpha,
the packed new map, original geometry counts, and no missing dependencies. Blender
5.2.1 LTS performed generation and rendering without external textures or caches.

`renders/water-v005/verification.json` provides the reopen and reload receipt.
`renders/water-v005/water-material-pilot.png` is a Blender material preview,
not evidence of website appearance, reflection synchronization, or device performance.
The browser integration task validates those independently.
